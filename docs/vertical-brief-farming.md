# Farming and agriculture, UK-first: vertical brief

Title candidate, researched 18 Sep 2026. Same structure as the fleet, gym/fitness,
golf resorts, barbering and airports briefs: market, competitors, demand,
advertisers, risk, verdict. Scope as briefed: a business title for farm owners and
managers and agri-business (profitability, subsidies/ELMs/SFI, machinery, inputs,
labour, diversification, succession, tax/APR/IHT, land, tech).

## 0. Verdict

**Conditional yes, and only as a narrower title than the one briefed. A general
"farm business" title would be a head-on fight with the strongest trade press we
have ever measured (Farmers Weekly at Tranco 50,669, stronger than every airport
incumbent), and it would lose. The open seam is the farm as a land business: the
31% of farm income that now comes from diversification, plus the new land-use
money (solar and battery leases, biodiversity net gain, carbon, holiday lets,
venues, farm retail, letting buildings) and the tax and succession decisions that
hang off it.**

Four findings drive the conditional yes.

1. **The money is real and moving toward our seam.** UK Total Income from
   Farming was **£8.4bn in 2025 (+20.5%)**, bank lending to agriculture reached
   **£18.8bn** (year to March 2026, +~10%), farmland holds at **~£8,873/acre**
   for grade 3 arable (Savills, Feb 2026). More importantly for the seam: **72% of
   English farms now have diversified income, averaging £22,400 and 31% of farm
   business income** (Defra Farm Business Survey 2024/25), letting buildings is the
   single most profitable enterprise on the average farm (£27,900), and 27% of
   farms now generate solar. The BNG unit market roughly tripled to an estimated
   **£93m in 2026** *(source is a vendor blog, unverified)*.
2. **The land-use money SERPs are held by lead-gen microsites, not publishers.**
   "Solar farm lease per acre", "battery storage lease per acre", "BNG unit
   prices" and "farm diversification ideas" are owned by one-product lead-gen
   sites (SolarGridCheck, LandBNG, livedin.co.uk), vendors, law firms and land
   agents. Farmers Weekly appears in some of them but does not own them, and a
   one-man site (britfarmers.com, absent from Tranco) ranks for "SFI 2026 payment
   rates". That last point cuts both ways (§3) but proves a new domain can rank.
3. **Advertiser proof is the deepest we have seen, and the seam has its own
   show.** LAMMA 2026 had **800+ exhibitors and 45,000+ visitors**, Cereals 2026
   **650+ exhibitors**, Groundswell 2026 **375**, the Royal Highland Show and Great
   Yorkshire Show **1,000+ trade stands each**. Critically, the **Farm Business
   Innovation Show (NEC, November) is a 250+ exhibitor show dedicated to
   diversification** (Environment Bank, Canopy & Stars, Sykes, finance, container,
   solar, glamping and planning firms). That is the seam's advertiser list,
   already paying.
4. **News supply is effectively unlimited.** Every core farm-business query we
   ran hit Google News' 100-item cap inside seven days. The wire will never go
   hungry; the problem is the opposite one, that most of it is the incumbents'
   ground (§5).

The three things that could kill it, in order:

- **The incumbents are the strongest field we have ever faced and they already
  cover business.** Farmers Weekly (50,669), Agriland (98,680 for .ie),
  Irish Farmers Journal (129,501), FarmingUK (226,667), Farmers Guardian
  (288,781), plus AHDB (111,308) and NFU (135,219) as free publishers. Farmers
  Weekly has a dedicated Business section with finance, succession and
  "alternative land uses" sub-sections. Drift into SFI news, commodity prices or
  machinery and we lose permanently.
- **Politics and YMYL.** The APR/IHT change is the defining farm story of
  2025-26 and is politically charged (protests, Clarkson, Lord Blunkett calling
  it a "mistake" this week). Tax, succession and lease advice are YMYL-adjacent
  money decisions for readers with £2m+ estates.
- **Weak internal asset and some collision.** CIM has no farming title. The
  crossover is partial (Hotel/Bar Magazine for farm venues, holiday lets and farm
  retail; MEPCA for agri-engineering), and pickups/agri vehicles collide with
  The Fleet Magazine (§7, §9).

This is the same shape as airports' non-aero revenue: a third of the profit, the
fastest-growing third, and nobody writes it as money journalism for the owner.

### Pain points with citable numbers (live in Sep 2026)

- **APR/BPR from 6 April 2026**: 100% relief capped at the first £2.5m of
  combined agricultural and business property (transferable between spouses, so
  £5m for a couple); 50% relief above that, i.e. an effective 20% IHT rate.
  Political reversal pressure is live (UFU pre-Budget submission, Blunkett,
  Countryside Alliance "rural reset").
- **SFI26**: 71 of 102 previous actions returned; Window 2 open now; SFI spend
  up to £738m as direct (BPS delinked) payments fall; FW reports two-thirds of
  eligible farms could miss out.
- **Machinery restructuring**: record-low tractor registrations and a "growing
  number of farm kit sales" (Velcourt dispersal, Cheffins) signal consolidation.
- **Inputs**: fertiliser price and six-week delivery delays; red diesel at ~70p
  in the NAAC baseline with surcharges advised.
- **Succession**: 48% of UK farmers have no succession plan, static for three
  years (NFU Mutual-cited figure via Alan Boswell *(unverified)*).

### Seasonality

Year-round news, but the commercial calendar is lumpy and predictable: LAMMA
(Jan), SFI windows (spring/autumn), Cereals and Royal Highland (Jun), Groundswell
and Great Yorkshire (Jul), harvest (Jul-Sep, quieter business news), UK Dairy Day
(Sep), Budget (Nov), Farm Business Innovation Show (Nov). Tax and succession
content peaks Oct-Apr (Budget to tax year end). Diversification (glamping, venues,
farm retail) peaks Jan-Apr planning season. Plan the evergreen calendar around
these, as golf does around the season.

### Calibration: the honest read

Fleet's incumbents sat at 2.5-2.8m and we beat them. Airports ran 102k-645k and we
called it viable only via the seam. **Farming's top incumbent is at 50k, twice as
strong as anything in the airports set, and it already covers farm business.**
Farmers Guardian (289k) and FarmingUK (227k) alone would be the strongest
competitors any of our titles faces. There is no version of a general farming or
farm-business title that wins search against this field.

Mapped by what each actually defends: FW = everything, weekly, with business as a
large section; FG = news, livestock, awards; FarmingUK = press-release news; AHDB
= data and benchmarking; NFU = policy; Agriland/IFJ = Ireland. **What nobody
defends: owner-facing, web-first money journalism on non-farming income from
farmland**, the solar, BESS, BNG, carbon, lets, venues, retail and storage
decisions, priced, compared and benchmarked. Farm Diversity is the only title
aimed at it and is absent from Tranco with roughly monthly output. CLA's Land &
Business covers the landowner reader but is a members-only print organ.

## 4. The contamination problem

Lower than golf or barbering, but not zero, and it lives in specific stems.

- **"farm shop"**: measured 55 items/7d, **~0% B2B**: "best farm shop in
  Northumberland", pumpkin patches, Molly-Mae, "priciest farm shop". The rare
  usable item (HATCH/Smallford backdated business rates) is a business rates
  story, not a farm-shop story.
- **"holiday let farm"**: ~20% usable; mostly property listings and local
  planning.
- **"farm estate for sale" / "land agent farm"**: ~30%; property porn and
  Clarkson's Farm celebrity.
- **"farmers"** (bare): ~30% usable; protests, crime, deaths, human interest,
  choirs.
- **Clarkson's Farm** is a standing consumer vector: Cereals 2026 was held at
  Diddly Squat and his name appears across unrelated feeds.
- **Smallholder/hobby intent**: "1 acre solar farm", "how much to rent an acre",
  "smallholding for sale" pull lifestyle buyers.

**Rules:**

> **The owner rule (farming edition).** Every article is written for someone who
> owns, manages, finances or advises a farm or rural land business. If the
> natural reader is a shopper, a tourist, a Clarkson viewer or a smallholding
> dreamer, it is not our article. No farm-shop reviews, no days out, no property
> listings, no celebrity farming.

> **Never seed the engine with the bare queries `farmers`, `farm shop`,
> `farm for sale` or `farm subsidies`** (the last is dominated by US farm bill
> and global items). Scoped queries only, per §5.

## 6. Audience: the honest sizing

- **Core: farm business owners and decision-makers.** ~168,000 farmers,
  partners, directors and spouses in England alone (June 2025), 446,000 UK
  agricultural workforce, ~94,700 farm businesses in the FBS population
  *(scope unverified)*, 25,834 NI farms. **72% have diversified**, so the seam
  reaches most of the core, not a fringe.
- **Landowners and estates**: CLA's 28,000-33,000 members own or manage 10m
  acres, over half of rural land in England and Wales. These are the readers
  with solar, BESS, BNG and lettings decisions and the largest APR exposure.
- **Advisers**: land agents (Savills, Strutt & Parker, Carter Jonas, Knight
  Frank, Brown & Co, Berrys, Cheffins, Stags), rural solicitors (Thrings,
  Wilsons, Roythornes, Knights, Weightmans), rural accountants (Old Mill, Albert
  Goodman, Saffery, PKF Francis Clark, MHA, Brown & Co), ICAEW Farming and Rural
  Business group. Readers and advertisers at once.
- **Supply chain**: LAMMA alone drew 45,000+ visitors; Cereals 25,000 expected;
  Farm Business Innovation Show ~18,000 expected *(organiser estimate)*.

Honest read: **the largest reader universe of any title we have assessed**
(fleet was large, this is larger and more homogeneous in its money problems),
UK-first with a natural Ireland extension.

**Reader definition to lock before launch:** farm owner-occupier · tenant farmer
(FBT) · estate owner/manager · diversification manager · land agent · rural
solicitor/accountant · agri-lender · energy/natural-capital developer's land
team · farm retail/venue/lettings operator (business side only).

## 8. Tools we can build

All reduce to the house primitive: a structured dataset with scheduled
enrichment. Ranked by "creates a proprietary, citable number".

1. **Land-Use Income Index (flagship).** Quarterly, sourced ranges for solar
   rent/acre, BESS rent/MW, BNG unit prices by habitat, woodland/carbon,
   building-let rents, glamping pitch yields, FBT rents. Every SERP in §3 marked
   "very winnable" becomes a landing page. Today the numbers come from lead-gen
   sites with a conflict of interest; an independent, dated, sourced index is
   the product.
2. **Farmland energy and BNG project tracker.** Every solar, BESS and habitat
   bank consent and refusal in the UK (from planning portals and the BNG
   register): size, developer, status. Feeds the news desk (each consent is a

## 9. Editorial risk

Four rules.

1. **The owner rule** (§4). Load-bearing. No consumer, tourist, celebrity or
   hobby content.
2. **The advice rule (YMYL).** APR/IHT, succession, tenancy and lease terms are
   high-value money decisions. We report the rules, the numbers and named
   advisers' views; we never tell a reader what to do with their estate. Every
   tax piece carries the rule's source (HMRC, Commons Library, Finance Act) and
   date, and is re-reviewed when rules change (the APR reversal campaign means
   they may). Lease and BNG figures are ranges with the source named, never one
   number in our own voice (same as the airports scope-and-figure rule).
3. **The politics rule.** The farm tax, solar-on-farmland fights, protests and
   Clarkson are politically charged. Business stories only: who pays, how much,
   what it changes for the owner. No editorial line on the government, the
   protests or "food security versus solar".
4. **The farm-incident rule.** Farm deaths (the slurry-tank case in this week's
   feed), rural crime and farmer mental health are real and frequent in the
   feed. We do not cover fatalities or crime as news. Mental health is covered
   only as a business-support topic with named charities (RABI, Farming Help,
   Yellow Wellies) and never generated as human-interest copy.

**Collision with our own titles (new, and specific to this vertical):**

- **The Fleet Magazine**: the double-cab pickup BIK change and agri-vehicle
  finance are Fleet's ground. Rule: Fleet owns vehicle tax and fleet
  management; the farm title covers vehicles only as farm capex, and never
  targets pickup/BIK queries.
- **Smart SME**: "how to start a farm shop", "farm diversification ideas" in
  its generic form, and rural business rates are Smart SME-shaped queries.
  **Check the keyword registry before commissioning** (per the Fleet keyword
  registry memory: the engine has no memory of target queries and Smart SME
  already cannibalised Fleet twice).
- **MEPCA**: agri-engineering manufacturing news belongs to MEPCA.
