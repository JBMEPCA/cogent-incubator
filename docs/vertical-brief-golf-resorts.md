# Golf resorts, global — vertical brief

Title #3 candidate, researched 18 Aug 2026. Same structure as the fleet business
case and the gym/fitness brief: market, competitors, demand, advertisers, risk,
verdict. Sponsor: Dec (Hotel Magazine publication manager).

## 0. Verdict

**Yes — and on the evidence this is the strongest of the three verticals we have
assessed. Launch it as a golf *resort* business title, global, in English.**

Three findings drive that.

1. **The incumbents are weak in a way the gym incumbents were not.** Golf Inc, the
   self-described "world's only magazine for golf course developers and owners",
   sits at Tranco **2,487,655** — the same band as the fleet titles we decided we
   could beat. The strongest pure golf-B2B site in the world, Golf Business News,
   is at **522,212**. There is no Health Club Management in this sector.
2. **CIM already owns the adjacent advertiser book.** Hotel Magazine and Bar
   Magazine are ours. A golf resort is a hotel with a very expensive garden, and
   Dec already sells to the hospitality half of its supplier base. No previous
   title launched into an advertiser list we had partial access to on day one.
3. **The money is moving right now.** KSL closed on Invited Clubs at **$2.6–3.0bn**
   on 9 June 2026. Institutional capital is repricing the asset class, and the
   trade press covering it has no domain authority.

The two things that would kill it are both manageable and both must be designed in
before launch, not after: **consumer contamination**, which is materially worse
here than in fitness, and a **narrow true reader universe** that has to be widened
deliberately from "resort GMs" to the whole travel-and-development supply chain.

### Golf tourism — the number to be careful with

Published market sizes for "golf tourism" range from **$6.9bn to $30.6bn for 2026**
depending on whether the analyst counts only golf-specific spend or the whole trip.
IMARC's series — $27.2bn in 2025 rising to $44.3bn at 5.4% CAGR — is the most
commonly cited. **Do not print a single figure as fact.** House rule: quote the
range, name the firm, or don't use it. This is the golf equivalent of fleet's
figure rule and it will come up constantly.

### Demand is at a genuine high

- **US**: rounds played hit an all-time record in 2025, the fourth time in five
  years, and 2026 was on pace for another at the midpoint — achieved with roughly
  2,000 *fewer* facilities than the 2000s peak. Total participation is 48m+, up 50%
  in a decade. More than 8m women and girls played on course for the first time.
- **Europe**: registered golfers up 3%+ to **over 4.9m** in 2025, +13% since 2020.
  Registered juniors up **13.6%** to 478,000+. 40.5m people played golf in Europe
  in 2025.

That combination — record demand against a shrinking facility count — is the
single best backdrop any of our three verticals has had. It means pricing power,
which means capital investment, which means advertising.

### Capital is consolidating

|---|---|---|
| KSL Capital acquires Invited Clubs (150+ properties, 300,000 members) | **$2.6–3.0bn** | closed 9 Jun 2026 |
| Arcis Golf | ~70 courses, EV $1.5–2.0bn | 2026 |
| Troon | 575+ 18-hole equivalents, largest in world | 2026 |
| Heritage Golf (KSL) | 6 → 47 clubs since 2020 | 2026 |

### Development pipeline — the global angle earns its keep here

- **Saudi Arabia**: Vision 2030 targets a *tripling* of the national course count.
  Qiddiya (opening 2026), Trump International at Wadi Safar on a 2.6m m² site,
  Diriyah Golf & Equestrian Resort with Aman, Oberoi and Six Senses, Shura Links,
  Sindalah at NEOM.
- **Vietnam**: **50+ courses in planning or under construction** — the most active
  development market outside the US.
- **Iberia**: Portugal has committed **$95m** to modernising Algarve golf.

A UK-only title cannot cover any of that. A global one has a pipeline story every
week, and pipeline stories are what developers, architects and suppliers read.

**Calibration against the two briefs we already have.** Fleet's incumbents were at
2.5m–2.8m and we judged them beatable. Health Club Management was at 339,604 and
we called it eight times too strong to attack. Golf's *trade* press clusters at
458k–2.5m — i.e. between the two, and mostly toward the fleet end. **Golf Inc, the
title whose editorial remit overlaps ours most exactly, is at 2.49m: fleet
territory.** Meanwhile the consumer titles are at 18k–37k, which is Fleet News
(101,682) territory and then some.

That asymmetry is the whole strategic picture in one line: **the trade is
undefended and the consumer ground is fortified.** Every editorial decision follows
from staying on the correct side of it.

### The seam nobody occupies

Map the trade set by what it actually covers:

- **Golf Inc** — developers, owners, transactions. Quarterly. Weak domain.
- **Golf Course Industry / GCM / BIGGA** — agronomy, turf, maintenance.
- **Club + Resort Business** — US private clubs, some resorts.
- **NGCOA Golf Business** — US course owners, association organ.
- **Golf Management (Portman)** — EMEA clubs, quarterly print, invisible online.
- **Golf Business News** — daily trade news, genuinely global, no features depth,
  and it explicitly refuses sponsored posts.

Nobody runs **the golf resort as a hospitality P&L**: the property that has to fill
rooms *and* a tee sheet, sell F&B, run a spa, land group and MICE business, manage
water and agronomy costs, and answer to an owner who now increasingly is a private
equity fund. That is the seam. It is also precisely where Hotel Magazine's
advertisers already live.

## 3. Who actually owns the searches

Same test as the fitness brief, and the answer is the same only more so.

| Query tested | Who ranks |
|---|---|
| "how to market a golf resort" | Inquivix, TriMark Digital, Trigger Digital, Cvent, GolfBack, Keiser University — **agencies and vendors, zero publishers** |
| "golf course management software" | GetApp, SourceForge, wifitalents.com, tezeract.ai, Guideflow — **directories and AI content farms** |
| "how much does it cost to build a golf course" | thecostguys.com, latestcost.com, currentcost.org, designtransitionstudio.com, selector.kurlon.com — **generated cost-estimator slop** |
| "dynamic pricing golf course" | Teesnap, Golfmanager, Priswing, GolfBack, Sagacity Golf — **vendor blogs** |

Three consequences.

1. **The SERP is winnable, and by a wider margin than fitness.** In fitness the
   vendors holding the SERP at least had real products and competent content. Here,
   a commercially significant query — the cost of building a golf course, a
   question with eight-figure decisions behind it — is held by auto-generated
   estimator sites. Competent, sourced editorial displaces that.
2. **Every vendor on that list is a prospective advertiser.** A company running a
   content-marketing programme has a proven budget and a proven belief in the
   audience. Competitor list and advertiser list are the same list, again.
3. **The incumbents are not competing for these queries at all.** Golf Inc and
   Golf Business News were absent from every commercial query tested. They are
   print-and-newsletter businesses with websites attached.

## 4. The contamination problem — the biggest single risk

In fleet, "BiK rate 2026/27" had no consumer twin. In fitness, "gym near me" was a
twin and we wrote a rule for it. **In golf, the consumer twin is the entire
category, and it is defended by domains 100× stronger than anything we will build.**

I measured it rather than assumed it. Pulling the live Google News feed for
`golf resort` — the obvious query anyone would seed — the top results were:

- Laguna Golf Phuket appoints new Director of Agronomy *(B2B — usable)*
- Why two golf courses in Surrey could be forced to close *(B2B — usable)*
- Trump Turnberry adds new 12-hole course *(B2B — usable)*
- Robotic mower technology at The Belfry *(B2B — usable)*
- Trump's Bombing Threats Have a Golf Course Problem — Mother Jones *(politics)*
- Inside ultimate bucket-list golf trip… — The Sun *(consumer travel)*
- Top Australian Golf Destinations: The Luxury Traveller's Guide *(consumer travel)*
- How To Properly Celebrate Fall On Cape Cod — Yahoo Life *(not golf)*
- Egypt's Madinaty to host World Golf Awards *(awards PR)*

**Roughly half the raw feed is unusable**, and the unusable half is exactly the
kind of content an unsupervised Researcher finds attractive. Seeded naively, the
engine will drift into consumer golf travel and lose to Golf Digest, GolfPass and
The Sun on their own ground, permanently.

**Rule to add to the editorial standard, alongside fleet's figure rule and
fitness's scope rule:**

> **The buyer rule.** Every article must be written for someone who is *paid* to
> be at a golf resort — owner, investor, GM, director of golf, course manager,
> developer, architect, tour operator, supplier. If the natural reader is someone
> on holiday, it is not our article. No course reviews, no "best of" destination
> lists, no travel guides, no tournament reporting except where it is a business
> story about the host venue.

| `golf course technology` | 0.14 | thin |

**The honest read: the business wire alone will not fill the day.** Summed and
de-duplicated, the business-intent queries yield roughly **3 usable items a day**
against a daily target that caps at 7. Fleet had the same shape and we solved it
with evergreen clusters; the same solution applies, but it must be planned rather
than discovered in week three.

The mitigation is that golf's *non-news* supply is unusually rich:

- **Trade bodies publishing hard data on a schedule** — R&A (*Golf Around the
  World*, biennial), NGF (Graffis Report, monthly rounds-played), EGA/R&A European
  Participation Report, GCSAA, BIGGA, IAGTO, PGA of America. Each release is a
  guaranteed article with a citable number.
- **A working newswire already exists** — The Golf Wire, plus Business Wire,
  PR Newswire and GlobeNewswire golf tags, plus USGA and R&A media centres.
- **Show calendars** — every show below is a pre-schedulable content block.
- **Development pipeline** — LINKS publishes a *manual* seasonal round-up of
  courses in development. That is a tool waiting to be built (see §8).

## 6. Audience — the honest sizing

This is the weakest part of the case and needs to be faced directly.

**Narrow definition** — properties that are genuinely "golf resorts": one directory
lists 448 across 30 countries. Realistically the global universe is low thousands.
As a reader base that is too small on its own.

**The addressable audience is the supply chain, not the properties:**

| Segment | Size | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Golf courses worldwide | 38,081 | R&A/NGF |
| IAGTO members (operators, resorts, courses, boards, airlines) in 94 countries | **2,461** | IAGTO |
| PGA Show attendees | 34,000+ | 2026 |
| GCSAA Conference attendees | 12,824 (+15%) | 2026 |
| BTME delegates | 5,000 | 2026 |
| Golf Inc's own event audience | ~500 leading operators/developers | Golf Inc |

IAGTO is the most useful single number in this brief: **2,461 accredited members
across 94 countries, controlling an estimated 85% of golf holiday packages sold
worldwide and €1bn+ of annual turnover.** That is a defined, named, reachable,
globally distributed B2B audience with budget — and IAGTO's own domain is at 1.33m,
so it is not serving them digitally.

**Reader definition to lock before launch:**
owner/investor · resort GM · director of golf · course manager/superintendent ·
developer · architect · golf tour operator · destination marketing organisation ·
hotel group golf lead · supplier.

## 8. Tools we can build

Ranked by "creates a proprietary, citable number" — the thing that gets a
low-authority domain linked to by high-authority ones.

1. **Golf Resort Rate Index (build first).** Scrape published green fees and
   package rates across a fixed basket of destinations — Algarve, Costa del Sol,
   Belek, Scotland, Ireland, Florida, Arizona, Dubai, Vietnam, Thailand — and
   publish a monthly index with currency-adjusted YoY movement. Nobody publishes
   this. It is cheap, it is automatable, it produces a fresh number every month,
   and a monthly number is the single most link-attracting object a small
   publisher can own. It also gives the Newsletter Manager a guaranteed lead item.
2. **Global development pipeline tracker.** Courses announced, under construction,
   and opening, with country, architect, developer and status. LINKS does this
   *manually* twice a year. A live, filterable, permanently-updated version beats
   it outright and makes us the reference for every "golf development in X" query.
3. **Transaction tracker.** Golf course and resort sales with price, buyer, seller,
   date. Golf Inc publishes an annual "top sales" list. A live one, in a market
   where KSL just spent $3bn, becomes the sector's record of itself.
4. **Ownership and management graph.** Which management company runs which
   property, under which owner. This is the dataset that makes consolidation
   stories writable in minutes and is a natural backlink magnet.
5. **Water and regulation tracker.** Irrigation restrictions, reclaimed-water
   mandates and drought rules by jurisdiction. High editorial value, genuinely
   useful, and it puts us on the right side of the sector's most contentious issue.
6. **Show and tournament-host calendar.** Feeds the content plan and the ad calendar.

Tools 1–4 all reduce to the same primitive: **a structured property database with
scheduled enrichment.** Build that once.

## 9. Editorial risk

Lower than fitness — there is no YMYL exposure — but three specific rules are
needed.

1. **The buyer rule** (§4). Load-bearing. Without it the title drifts consumer and
   dies.
2. **The market-size rule.** Golf tourism valuations vary by more than 4× between
   research firms. Always name the source and the year, or quote a range. Never
   assert a single market size in our own voice.
3. **The geopolitics rule.** Saudi PIF money is threaded through this entire
   sector — LIV, Vision 2030 course development, tour sponsorship — and the
   sportswashing debate is live and unresolved (PIF announced it will stop funding
   LIV after the 2026 season). Trump-owned properties are simultaneously golf
   venues and US political stories. **We cover these as business stories only:
   capital, capacity, bookings, contracts. We do not editorialise on human rights
   or US domestic politics.** An AI-written title with no named political
   correspondent has no business taking a side, and taking one costs us advertisers
   on both flanks.

A fourth, softer note: the environmental critique of golf — water, land, chemicals
— is legitimate and growing (Spanish courses are under active investigation for
illegal abstraction; Extinction Rebellion has targeted courses). Covering it
seriously is an editorial *advantage*, because the incumbents are too close to
their advertisers to do it well. Cover it straight and it becomes a differentiator.

## 12. Before committing

1. **Talk to Dec first, and get three things in writing**: which Hotel Magazine
   advertisers he believes cross over, whether any would take a launch slot, and
   which of his contacts would write or be quoted. His network is the asset that
   makes this vertical better than fitness — the brief above is worth less than
   one named advertiser who says yes.
2. **Ahrefs, one month, ~£99.** Same call as fleet and fitness. Here it has one
   specific job beyond sizing: **separate trade intent from travel intent** before
   the section taxonomy is locked. Priority clusters — resort operations and P&L,
   development and construction, transactions and ownership, agronomy and water,
   golf travel trade (operators, not travellers), technology and revenue
   management.
3. **Register `golfresortbusiness.com` and `.co.uk` now.** Both are available and
   the cost of losing the name exceeds the cost of holding it.
4. **The Smart SME gate is unchanged** — inbound press-release flow plus one paying
   advertiser. Nothing here overrides it.
5. **Playbook debt specific to this title:**
   - `lib/news-searches.js` is hardcoded `hl=en-GB&gl=GB&ceid=GB:en`. A global
     title needs **multi-locale news searches** (at minimum en-GB, en-US, en-AU)
     or it will systematically miss 42% of the world's courses. This is new debt
     that fleet and fitness did not expose.
   - The `seed-news-searches.mjs` tenant-scoping fix from fleet is still
     outstanding.
   - A `GOLF_NEWS_SEARCHES` block is needed — and per §4 it must be seeded with
     **business-intent queries only**. Do not seed `golf resort`.
   - The buyer rule (§4), the market-size rule and the geopolitics rule need adding
     to `docs/editorial-standard.md` before the first draft is generated.
6. **One decision needed from JB:** resort-focused (recommended — it is the
   unoccupied seam and it is where Dec's book is) or the broader "business of golf"
   (bigger audience, but puts us head-on against Golf Business News, the one
   incumbent that is actually competent).
