How these numbers are built

Per article

Editor draft on Opus (~8k in / 6k out incl. thinking) $0.19, QA gate $0.05, Designer routing on Haiku plus two Opus vision checks $0.04 — about $0.28 clean, ~$0.45 with QA-hold retries. Optimised assumes prompt caching on the invariant house-style and editorial-standard blocks.

Agent overhead

Director $27/mo at today's every-tick cadence, Researcher $7.50, SEO $6, Backlink $3, LinkedIn $2.40, Finance $2, Newsletter $0.20. Optimised event-gates the Director (13 ticks/day to 3) and moves it to Sonnet 5.

Newsletter delivery

Per contact follows Mailchimp Standard, billed on total contacts across the whole account — published tiers stop around 200k, above which it is negotiated and this model extrapolates at £5.50 per thousand. Per send is Amazon SES at $0.10 per thousand emails, weekly issues, plus £40 a month for dedicated IPs and monitoring.

Everything else

  • SiteGround stepped by site count: GoGeek → Cloud Entry → Cloud Business
  • Vercel Pro + compute, Neon, domains at £11/yr
  • Five minutes per title per day of approvals, plus a context-switch penalty; the fleet queue assumes 90 seconds per title against a fixed ten-minute review
  • Converted at £1 = $1.27

One caveat that outranks all of this. costOf() in lib/agents/registry.js matches model IDs by exact string and falls back to Opus pricing on a miss, while recordUsage() passes the ID the API returns. If those disagree, every Haiku and Sonnet call in Smart SME is currently billed at 5× in your own reporting — so the first job is fixing that lookup and replacing these estimates with 30 days of measured AgentRun data.
