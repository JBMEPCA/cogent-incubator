## Reader

The brief's own example, adopted verbatim: **the operations director of a
40-vehicle haulier**. Widened to fleet director, fleet manager and transport
manager, across fleets from **10 vehicles to 250+**.

They read to keep up with launches, regulation and what comparable operators
are doing, in order to cut cost, raise quality and stay compliant.

**Not our reader:** the general public chasing cheap cars or car tax,
performance and lifestyle motoring, students researching the motor industry.

## The formula, which is unusually clear here

All four article answers point at one shape: **a change, plus what it costs the
business.** Not the announcement, the consequence.

- Favourite: *HGV Road User Levy 2026: Rates, Who Pays and How It's Calculated*.
  Something a fleet operator must know and would not otherwise.
- *Toyota Hilux two-seat conversion* — a vehicle announcement carried into tax
  and business consequence.
- *Double cab pickup tax rules: what changed and what it costs* — the
  "what does this mean for me" article.
- *Best Electric Vans for UK Fleets in 2026* — a buyer's shortlist that answers
  a question someone actually types.

Commission against that test: **does the reader learn what it costs them?**

**Never:** consumer buying advice, "best cars" content for the general public.
And a hard accuracy rule, in his words: anything on **tax or legislation has to
be 100% factual**. That is the strictest editorial constraint any sheet has
produced so far and it should be a gate, not a preference.

## Search queries, and they are good ones

Unlike the airport sheet, these are directly usable long-tail:

- "what is the best van for a small/large business"
- "tax rules on company cars/fleets"
- "how much does it cost to run an electric van"

## The engine is already doing this

Worth telling Lucas: one of his three exemplars (*Best Electric Vans for UK
Fleets 2026*) is our own article, and *Salary Sacrifice Petrol and Hybrid
Company Cars: The 2026/27 Tax Rules* already answers his second Google query
almost word for word. The formula he describes is the one the title is running.
The gap he names is the real one, and it is not a topic gap.

## The gap he identifies (and it is the right one)

> "A lot of the news currently is just what people have launched, what the
> government have announced."

He wants **real costs, real case studies, and interviews with senior people**.
That is precisely what the interview pipeline exists to produce, and it is also
what his advertisers say they want to sit beside. Fleet should be next after
Smart SME for SME-Leaders-style profiles.

## COLLISION: Smart SME is publishing fleet stories

The registry risk is live, not theoretical. Smart SME has published:

| Smart SME article | Keyphrase | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Crystal Ball Tops a UK Fleet Platform List | `UK fleet platform` | squarely Fleet's beat |
| SMMT Calls for Van Mandate Rethink | `van mandate review` | SMMT is Fleet's trade body |
| Used EV Sales Hit 1 in 18 as SMMT Presses for ZEV Mandate Rethink | `used EV sales record share` | same |

Two titles bidding for the same queries splits the authority that should be
concentrated in one. Fix before commissioning more: fleet vehicles, SMMT, van
and EV-operations queries belong to Fleet; Smart SME keeps them only where the
angle is genuinely "small business owner", not "fleet operator".
