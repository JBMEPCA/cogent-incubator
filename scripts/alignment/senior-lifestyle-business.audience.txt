The people who develop, fund and run retirement communities where residents have their own front door: operator chief executives, finance directors and heads of department, development and land directors, sales and marketing directors, village general managers, investors, REITs, private equity, fund managers, banks and lenders, planners, architects, council commissioners, and the advisers and suppliers who serve them. They read us to see what is changing across the sector and what their peers are doing with it: new legislation, what other villages and communities are building, where to put the next pound of time or money, and which product or partner is worth a look. A knowledge hub for how the global senior living and retirement community industry is evolving, written from the perspective of fellow providers. UK-led, with Australia and New Zealand as the second market and the US as the benchmark the UK should be learning from. Never the resident or their family, and never the care home, which belongs to CIM's Care Home Magazine
