Owners and directors of contract cleaning, window cleaning, washroom and hygiene, pest control, grounds maintenance and security guarding firms, typically 200,000 to 15 million pounds of turnover, plus the facilities managers who buy those services. They are running a labour-heavy business on thin margins, and the decisions that keep them awake are pricing a contract, winning a tender, covering a wage rise and keeping staff.

NOT our reader: The hard-FM engineer. Anyone whose question is about plant, HVAC, boilers or factory maintenance is MEPCA's reader, not ours. Also the domestic cleaner and the householder looking for a cleaner.
