# FM Weekly: editorial standard

The business of clean.

## Who this is for

Owners and directors of contract cleaning, window cleaning, washroom and hygiene, pest control, grounds maintenance and security guarding firms, typically 200,000 to 15 million pounds of turnover, plus the facilities managers who buy those services. They are running a labour-heavy business on thin margins, and the decisions that keep them awake are pricing a contract, winning a tender, covering a wage rise and keeping staff.

**Not our reader.** The hard-FM engineer. Anyone whose question is about plant, HVAC, boilers or factory maintenance is MEPCA's reader, not ours. Also the domestic cleaner and the householder looking for a cleaner.

## The rules that decide what this title publishes

### 1. **Soft FM only.** Hard FM, meaning plant, HVAC, boilers, engineering and factory maintenance, belongs to MEPCA and must never be covered here. The masthead says FM because that is what an advertiser buys; the content stays on cleaning and service firms because that is where the search is winnable. If a piece is about a boiler, it is the wrong title.

### 2. **Write for the firm, not the cleaner.** Every piece answers a question an owner or an FM buyer has about money, contracts, people or risk. Never cleaning technique, product dilution or method.

### 3. **Labour law and immigration are part of the beat and carry legal risk.** Right to work, illegal working penalties and modern slavery in supply chains are real stories here. Attribute every rule to the Home Office, HMRC, HSE or Acas by name, never state the law in our own voice, and never tell a reader whether they are compliant.

### 4. **Never quote a charge-out rate as a market fact.** Every published cleaning rate comes from someone selling cleaning or selling to cleaners, and none of them says what is included. Build rates up from cost, attribute every vendor range as that vendor's claim, and say plainly that published figures disagree.
