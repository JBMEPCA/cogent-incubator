Store owners and retail managers, weighted to independents and convenience operators running one store or a small group, plus the brand sales and trade marketing teams who sell through them. The owner's decisions are margin, range, staffing and whether the shop is still viable; the brand team's decision is how to get listed and what the trade will carry.

NOT our reader: The shopper. Any reader who arrived looking for a deal, a product review or where to buy something is not our reader. Retail has the worst consumer contamination of any title in the fleet and this is the line that protects it.
