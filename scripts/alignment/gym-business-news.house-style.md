# Gym Business News house style

Write like someone who built a gym or a PT business from nothing and learned
the money side the hard way, talking to an owner who is where they were three
years ago. Not a fitness influencer, not a consultant, not a head-office
strategy deck. The reader runs classes or sessions all day and reads between
them, often on a phone.

- Plain, direct, second person: "you", "your gym", "your studio". Contractions
  are fine. Short sentences.
- Lead with the money, the decision or the change. Never with scene-setting,
  and never with a statistic about how healthy the nation is.
- Say the point, then say why it matters, all the way to the money: more
  members kept, more revenue per member, less cost per square metre.
- Money in pounds for UK pieces; dollars or euros only where the story is
  genuinely international, and never mixed within one comparison.
- Costs and prices as attributed, dated ranges. Never a single invented figure.
- "Member" is the customer. "Operator" is the business. A self-employed PT is
  a business owner, not staff; write to them as one.
- The provider rule: name the real companies in any buying guide or comparison
  (software, payments, equipment, insurance, finance), and say who each suits.
  A guide that names nobody is no use to the reader or to the advertisers who
  want to sit beside it.
- No fitness-marketing voice: no "crush it", "beast mode", "transform",
  "unleash", "journey", "no excuses". No business-school voice either: no
  "leverage", "stakeholders", "synergies", "optimise", "solutions".
- No puns in headlines ("weighing up", "heavy lifting", "no pain no gain").
  They read consumer and they are beneath the title.
- Never write as if the reader were the member. "Your members" is right;
  "you'll feel stronger" is wrong.
- Specify imagery of real gyms, studios, kit and front desks, never stock
  photos of people mid-workout with a results feel to them.
- Every piece leaves the reader with something they can do this week.
- British spelling. No em dashes.
