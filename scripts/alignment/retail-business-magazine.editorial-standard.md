# Retail Business Magazine: editorial standard

Where brands meet the shelf.

## Who this is for

Store owners and retail managers, weighted to independents and convenience operators running one store or a small group, plus the brand sales and trade marketing teams who sell through them. The owner's decisions are margin, range, staffing and whether the shop is still viable; the brand team's decision is how to get listed and what the trade will carry.

**Not our reader.** The shopper. Any reader who arrived looking for a deal, a product review or where to buy something is not our reader. Retail has the worst consumer contamination of any title in the fleet and this is the line that protects it.

## The rules that decide what this title publishes

### 1. **Never a consumer title.** No shopper advice, no best-deals, no product reviews, no where-to-buy. Everything is written for the person running or supplying the shop. Any search carrying a supermarket's name is a shopper, not a trader, and that traffic is worth nothing to us.

### 2. **Age-restricted categories: report, never promote.** Tobacco, vapes, nicotine pouches and alcohol are core convenience lines, so the rules and the commercial effect on a shop are squarely in scope. Never describe such a product as appealing, never carry brand or product imagery for them, and never plan revenue on vape or nicotine advertising, which ends on 1 June 2027.

### 3. **The independent's P&L is the seam.** Convenience Store and Better Retailing cover product news and supplier promotions. Margin, rates, wages, crime, symbol group terms and selling the shop are what nobody writes for this reader, and they are what this title is for.

### 4. **Trade bodies are the sources and most have no feed.** ACS, BRC, BIRA and the Fed publish the figures this beat rests on and none of them is in the wire. Their reports are the anchor for the money stories; attribute every figure to the report and its date, and never quote a sector statistic without one.
