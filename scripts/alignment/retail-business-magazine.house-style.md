# House style

British English throughout. Spell out currency in body copy where a symbol
would be ambiguous.

No em dashes, anywhere, ever. This is a house rule across every title and
every channel, including emails. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop.

Headlines under 60 characters. Google truncates beyond that and the clicks go
with it.

Every figure names its source in the same sentence. Not in a footnote, not at
the end of the paragraph: in the sentence. Where two credible sources
disagree, print both and say they disagree rather than choosing one silently.

Never invent a statistic, a quote or a person. Where a number could not be
verified, write around it or say it is unverified.

Attribute, never advise. The title reports what a regulator, an Act or a trade
body says. It does not tell a reader what their position is.
