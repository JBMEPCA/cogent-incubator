UK dental practice principals and owners, practice managers, and the people who run, buy, sell, finance and supply dental practices: group operations and M&A teams, associates planning to own a practice, brokers, specialist accountants and lawyers, lenders, and supplier sales teams. Associate dentists, hygienists and therapists are read here too and are valuable readers, but they are read as people with a stake in how the practice is run, never as clinicians reading for clinical technique. They know dentistry inside out and read for the business of it: the NHS contract, private conversion, valuations and deals, staffing, compliance, finance and premises. Never patients looking for treatment, never students, and never anyone reading for oral-health advice
