Putting it in Gmail

  1. Select the block in the box above, from Tom Woollin down to and including the logo, and copy it.
  2. In Gmail, Settings, See all settings, General, scroll to Signature.
  3. Create new, name it Dental, then paste into the editor.
  4. Under Signature defaults set it for new emails and for replies.
  5. Save Changes at the bottom of the page.

Copying from this page keeps the logo and the link. Do not retype it.

Notes

  1. No email address or phone number in it, matching the other five titles. The From line carries the address, so this works on whichever mailbox Tom sends from. Say the word if you want either added.
  2. The logo is served from the app at /api/brand/logo/dental-business-news, not from the title site, because SiteGround's bot challenge refuses automated image fetches and the logo would break in some inboxes.
