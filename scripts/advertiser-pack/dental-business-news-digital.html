The title

Dental Business News launched on 18 September 2026. It covers the business of UK dentistry for the people who own practices, run them and sign for what goes into them: deals and consolidation, the NHS contract, regulation and compliance, premises and technology, finance and tax, private care and plans, and workforce.

Fifty articles were published in the first month, including a weekly interview series with named practice owners about how they actually run the business.

How we report

Every campaign is reported on its own figures: delivered, unique opens, unique clicks, and the click destinations. We do not reuse numbers between campaigns and we do not quote a house average in place of your result. If a campaign underperforms, the report will say so and we will talk about what to do next.
