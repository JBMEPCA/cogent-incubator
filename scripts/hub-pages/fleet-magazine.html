Company car and van tax

Benefit in kind is the figure that drives most fleet tax decisions, and it is calculated from the P11D value, the CO2 band and the driver's marginal rate. Start with how company car benefit in kind is calculated for 2026/27, then check whether your vehicles fall the other side of the line: a van is taxed on a flat van benefit charge rather than a percentage of list price, and double cab pickups moved from van treatment to car treatment, which changed the sums for a lot of operators.

Salary sacrifice remains the cheapest route into an electric company car, and the numbers are set out in our EV salary sacrifice explainer for 2026/27. The rules are different, and less generous, for combustion vehicles: see the 2026/27 tax rules for petrol and hybrid salary sacrifice. For a worked example of how one model lands in practice, we costed the BiK on a BYD Sealion 7.

Vehicle Excise Duty and road charges

Road tax stopped being a rounding error for van fleets when electric vehicles lost their exemption. Our breakdown of what UK fleets actually pay in van VED for 2026/27 covers the bands and the expensive-car supplement that now catches many EVs. Heavy operators should read it alongside the 2026 HGV Road User Levy rates and how the charge is calculated.

Then there are the charges that depend on where you drive rather than what you own. Clean Air Zone and congestion charges for 2026 sets out what each zone costs by vehicle class, which is the number that decides whether a non-compliant vehicle is worth keeping on urban routes.

Capital allowances and how you fund the vehicle

What you can write down, and when, often matters more than the headline price. Start with what fleets can claim in capital allowances on vans and trucks in 2026/27, then read what the Budget update changed for leased vans, because the treatment turns on the funding method rather than the vehicle.

That makes the funding decision a tax decision too. Contract hire, finance lease and outright purchase compared covers the trade-offs, and whole life cost versus P11D explains why the list price is the wrong basis for choosing between two vehicles once tax, depreciation and employer's National Insurance are counted.

Reimbursing mileage correctly

Reimburse above the published rate without evidence and you create a taxable benefit; reimburse below it and drivers are subsidising the business. HMRC Advisory Fuel Rates and the Advisory Electricity Rate covers both, including the awkward case of an EV driver charging at home. Employers running personal vehicles on business should also read the grey fleet duty of care, where the liability is not tax but it is real.

Emissions rules and the ZEV mandate

The ZEV mandate sets the share of zero-emission vehicles manufacturers must sell, which shapes what you can actually order and at what discount. The ZEV mandate explained for fleet operators is the starting point, and the government has since opened an early review with four options fleets need to watch. That review carries a residual value risk, which we covered when used EV sales hit a record one in eighteen.

Looking further out, the Euro 7 emissions standard changes specification and cost for vans and trucks from 2026, and fuel supply policy moves in the background: we track it through the DfT's RTFO fuel data releases.
